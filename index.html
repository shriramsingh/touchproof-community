Core Capabilities

Engineered for Seamless Daily Peace of Mind

Designed from the ground up for zero distraction, rapid 1-tap activation, and complete battery efficiency.

1-Tap Armored Touch Shield

Draws an ultra-thin, invisible protective barrier over running media players, YouTube videos, and GPS navigation. Media stays 100% visible while all swipes, taps, and gestures are completely blocked.

Smart In-Call Ear Guard

Automatically shields your display during active phone calls. Stops ear and cheek hangups, accidental mutes, or keypad dials on calls.

Dual-Sensor Pocket Fusion

Fuses proximity and accelerometer tilt sensors with a wake-lock window. Dims display inside pockets without triggering false blackouts on flat desks.

Pitch-Black OLED Mode

Turns pixels 100% black in pocket mode to physically turn off OLED/AMOLED pixels, significantly reducing battery consumption.

Quick Settings Tray Tile

Swipe down anywhere on Android to arm or disarm TouchProof directly from your system notification tray with 1 tap.

Permanent Bright Unlock

Floating Double-Tap or Triple-Tap badge remains clearly visible on screen so you can unlock comfortably without searching.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TouchProof, permissions, and privacy.

TouchProof draws an invisible, transparent protective overlay surface directly on top of running applications (such as YouTube, Spotify, and Netflix). This overlay intercepts all unwanted touches before they can pause, skip, or disturb your running apps.

Virtually zero. TouchProof uses event-based hardware sensors (proximity and accelerometer) that consume < 0.1% battery. When pocket mode is active on AMOLED/OLED screens, it renders 100% true black, saving battery compared to normal screen-on states.

Absolutely none. TouchProof has zero internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET is not even declared in the app manifest). The app contains no analytics SDKs, no trackers, and no ad frameworks.

Simply double-tap (or triple-tap) the floating unlock badge on your screen. You can drag and position the badge wherever your thumb naturally reaches, and feel distinct haptic vibration confirmation upon unlocking.

Yes! You can swipe down from anywhere to tap the Quick Settings notification tray tile, tap the 1x1 Home Screen widget, or long-press the app icon on your launcher.
