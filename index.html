Engineered for Everyday Peace of Mind

100% offline, privacy-first, zero battery drain when idle.

🔒

1-Tap Touch Armor

Instantly locks screen touches, swipes, and navigation gestures. Videos, GPS navigation, and recipes remain 100% visible while touch inputs are blocked.

📞

Smart In-Call Ear Guard

Automatically shields your display during active phone calls. Prevents accidental mute, hold, keypad presses, or hangups from your ear and cheek.

📱

Dual-Sensor Pocket Detection

Fuses proximity and ambient light sensors to detect pockets or bags. Dims backlight to save battery and wakes immediately when uncovered.

⚡

Quick Settings Tile

Toggle your touch shield directly from your Android notification tray with 1 tap, without opening the app.

🔓

Permanent Bright Unlock

Floating Double-Tap or Triple-Tap button stays permanently visible by default so you can unlock smoothly without searching.

🌐

100% Private & Offline

No internet permission is ever requested. Zero analytics, zero telemetry, zero advertising SDKs. Completely private.

Clean, Modern Jetpack Compose UI

Tested and optimized for Android 13 and Android 14+.
