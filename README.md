## 🛡️ About TouchProof

TouchProof is a lightweight, privacy-focused Android utility that draws an armored, transparent touch shield over your screen to prevent accidental touches, gestures, and cheek hangups during phone calls.

### Key Highlights:
- **1-Tap Lock:** Locks screen touches while keeping YouTube, Netflix, maps, or recipes 100% visible.
- **In-Call Ear Guard:** Protects voice calls against accidental mute, hold, keypad presses, or hangups from your cheek and ear.
- **Dual-Sensor Pocket Detection:** Automatically dims your display in pockets or bags to prevent mistouches and save battery.
- **Quick Settings Tile:** Lock touches directly from your Android notification tray with 1 tap.
- **100% Offline & Private:** Zero internet permission, zero analytics, zero trackers.
- **Native Multilingual:** Full localization in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Sanskrit (संस्कृतम्).
